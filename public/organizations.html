The framework

Six capability areas. One shared language for the whole org.

The same framework we use in individual coaching, applied at the team level — so an assessment finding maps directly to a development plan, not a generic competency list.

01 / Storytelling

Narratives that land

Can your people turn a project, a pitch, or a setback into a story a client or a board actually remembers?

02 / Leadership

Influence without the title

Where leadership is being demonstrated at every level already — and where it's being left on the table.

03 / Executive Presence

Credibility under pressure

How your people show up in the rooms that decide deals, promotions, and client confidence.

04 / Leadership through AI

Judgment, not outsourcing

Whether AI is sharpening your team's decisions or quietly replacing the thinking behind them.

05 / Critical Thinking

Past the first answer

Recommendations that hold up when a client, a VP, or a board member pushes back.

06 / Emerging Leaders

The first ninety days

Whether newly promoted managers are building trust and momentum, or quietly struggling alone.
