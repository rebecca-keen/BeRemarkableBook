# TikTok script pack — Be Remarkable

How to use these:
- Talking-head to camera. Keep each 25 to 45 seconds.
- The HOOK is the first line. Say it in the first 2 seconds AND put it as on-screen text. It decides whether anyone watches.
- One idea per video. End with a light CTA.
- Put your site link in your bio (TikTok only allows a bio link), and say "link in bio" or "free guides in my bio."
- Batch-film 4 to 5 in one sitting wearing different tops so it looks like different days.

Suggested caption formula: short restatement of the hook + 3 to 5 hashtags.
Hashtags to rotate: #careertok #genz #firstjob #newgrad #careeradvice #professionaldevelopment #worktok #careertips

## 1. The Gen Z trend (the one that sparked this)

HOOK: "Employers keep saying Gen Z can't communicate at work. It is not their fault."

SCRIPT:
"Employers keep saying Gen Z can't communicate at work. And here is what nobody adds. Companies cut the training and the mentorship that used to teach this stuff on the job. So a 22-year-old is expected to just know how to speak up in a meeting, handle a hard conversation, and read a room, with no one showing them how. That is not a personality problem. Communication is a skill. Which means it can be learned. If that is you, or you manage someone like that, I put free guides on exactly this in my bio. Start there."

ON-SCREEN TEXT BEATS: "It's not a personality problem" → "It's a skill" → "Skills can be built"

CAPTION: Gen Z isn't bad at communication. Nobody taught them, and companies stopped. #careertok #genz #newgrad #careeradvice

## 2. Nobody is training you anymore

HOOK: "Your company is not going to train you. Here is what to do about it."

SCRIPT:
"Your company is probably not going to train you the way they trained people ten years ago. Mentorship, feedback, someone pulling you aside to say try it this way, a lot of that quietly disappeared. So if you are waiting to be developed, you are going to wait a long time. The people who get ahead now develop themselves on purpose. Pick one skill this month. Storytelling, presence, speaking up. Practice it in real conversations. That is it. Free guides in my bio to start."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Waiting to be developed?" → "You'll wait a long time" → "Develop yourself on purpose"

CAPTION: If you're waiting to be trained, you'll wait forever. Develop yourself. #careertok #professionaldevelopment #worktok

## 3. The skill AI can't fake

HOOK: "AI can do almost anything now. Here is the one thing it cannot fake."

SCRIPT:
"AI can write the email, build the deck, summarize the meeting. So being good at those things is not the edge anymore. Everyone has the same tools. The one thing AI cannot fake is you in the room. Your judgment. The story only you lived. The way you make people trust you when the script runs out. That is the skill worth building right now, because it is the only one that is getting more valuable. Not less."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "AI raised the floor" → "Being polished isn't the edge" → "Being human is"

CAPTION: When everyone has AI, the human skills are the edge. #careeradvice #ai #professionaldevelopment #careertok

## 4. Tell a story in 3 sentences (practical, saveable)

HOOK: "You are not bad at interviews. You are just listing facts. Do this instead."

SCRIPT:
"If your answers feel forgettable, you are probably listing facts instead of telling a story. Here is the fix, in three sentences. One: here is how things were. Two: here is what I did and what changed. Three: here is why it mattered. That is it. Situation, your move, the result. Try it with something you did last month and say it out loud. It will feel completely different from reading a resume bullet. More like this in my bio."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "1. How things were" → "2. What you did + what changed" → "3. Why it mattered"

CAPTION: Save this for your next interview. Story in 3 sentences. #interviewtips #careertok #newgrad #storytelling

## 5. You're not a natural, you're untrained

HOOK: "You are not bad at presence. You are just untrained. Good news, that is fixable."

SCRIPT:
"People think presence is something you are born with. You either walk in and command the room or you do not. That is a myth, and it is holding you back. Presence is a set of behaviors. Preparing your one main point. Slowing down. Pausing instead of filling silence. Making eye contact that does not perform. Every one of those is trainable. So if you have decided you are just not a presence person, you are really just untrained. And untrained is fixable."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Presence isn't a personality" → "It's a set of behaviors" → "Behaviors are trainable"

CAPTION: You're not bad at presence. You're untrained. That's fixable. #careertok #executivepresence #professionaldevelopment
