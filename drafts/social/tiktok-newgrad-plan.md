# Be Remarkable — 10-Day Faceless TikTok Plan (New Grads / Job Seekers)

**The angle (from the trend):** employers say new grads coming out of school aren't ready, they cite communication and professionalism, and companies are offering less training while leaning on AI. The truth: this generation lost in-person years to COVID, walked into AI erasing entry-level tasks, and was never taught the human skills they're now judged on. The gap is real, it is NOT their fault, and every one of these skills can be built.

**Audience:** recent grads and early-career job seekers. Speak straight to them.
**Format:** faceless. Record each script as a voiceover, lay it over big on-screen text + simple visuals in CapCut.
**Every video ends with:** "free guides in my bio" (bio link = beremarkablebook.com/links).

---

## DAY 1 — Nobody prepared you (thesis)
HOOK: "Nobody prepared you for the job market you actually graduated into. And that is not on you."
VOICEOVER: "You did the degree. You did the internships you could get. And now employers are out here saying new grads can't communicate, aren't professional, aren't ready. But look at what happened. You lost years of in-person, learning-how-to-work time to COVID. Companies cut the training that used to bring people up to speed. And AI erased the easy entry-level tasks you'd normally learn on. So the thing you're being judged on now is the human skills, the exact stuff nobody ever taught you. The gap is real. It is also completely fixable."
ON-SCREEN: "You did everything right" → "COVID + no training + AI" → "Judged on skills nobody taught you" → "The gap is fixable"
B-ROLL: graduation cap / empty office / walking POV
CAPTION: New grads aren't the problem. The prep was. #newgrad #careertok #jobsearch #genz

## DAY 2 — What employers actually mean
HOOK: "Recruiters aren't rejecting you for your skills. It's usually this."
VOICEOVER: "When employers say new grads aren't ready, they don't mean your technical skills. They mean the human stuff. Following up. Speaking clearly in a meeting. Reading a room. Handling feedback without shutting down. Nobody teaches this in school, and companies stopped teaching it on the job. So it feels like a personality flaw. It's not. It's a set of skills, and they're learnable. That's the whole game right now."
ON-SCREEN: "Not your technical skills" → "It's communication + professionalism" → "Those are learnable"
B-ROLL: laptop / inbox / handshake
CAPTION: What employers mean when they say you're 'not ready.' #jobsearch #newgrad #careeradvice

## DAY 3 — Why your applications vanish (AI)
HOOK: "If your applications keep disappearing, here's what's actually happening."
VOICEOVER: "Everyone is using AI to write their resume and cover letter now. Which means every application looks the same. Polished, generic, forgettable. So the paper version of you no longer stands out, because a machine wrote everybody's. The place you can actually win is the moment a human gets involved. The interview. The story you tell. The way you show up. That's where AI stops helping everyone else and you can pull ahead."
ON-SCREEN: "AI wrote everyone's resume" → "On paper, you all blend" → "Win where a human is involved"
B-ROLL: stack of papers / chatbot screen / phone
CAPTION: Everyone's resume looks the same now. Win in the room. #jobsearch #newgrad #ai #careertok

## DAY 4 — Interview story in 3 sentences (SAVEABLE)
HOOK: "Bombing interviews even though you're qualified? You're listing facts. Do this."
VOICEOVER: "Qualified people bomb interviews because they list facts instead of telling a story. Fix it in three sentences. One, here's how things were. Two, here's what I did and what changed. Three, here's why it mattered. Situation, your move, the result. Take one thing from an internship or a project and tell it that way, out loud. It lands completely differently than reading bullet points off your resume."
ON-SCREEN: "1. How things were" → "2. What you did + what changed" → "3. Why it mattered"
B-ROLL: keep the 3 steps on screen (people screenshot this)
CAPTION: Save this before your next interview. #interviewtips #newgrad #jobsearch #storytelling

## DAY 5 — Great on paper, no offers
HOOK: "You look great on paper and still not getting offers? Here's the gap."
VOICEOVER: "If you're getting interviews but not offers, the problem usually isn't your qualifications. It's what happens in the room. You answer correctly, but forgettably. You have the knowledge but not the presence yet. Here's the shift. Stop trying to prove you're smart, they already invited you, they know. Start trying to be memorable. One clear point, one real story, said like you believe it. That's what turns a good candidate into the one they pick."
ON-SCREEN: "Interviews but no offers?" → "It's not your qualifications" → "It's presence, and that's learnable"
B-ROLL: office lobby / video-call grid
CAPTION: Great on paper, quiet in the room? Here's the fix. #jobsearch #interviewtips #newgrad

## DAY 6 — Your first 90 days
HOOK: "When you finally land the job, don't be the quiet new hire. Do this instead."
VOICEOVER: "The first ninety days set how people see you for years. Most new hires go quiet, head down, trying not to mess up. That's the mistake. You don't have to be loud. But pick one thing each week to contribute out loud. Ask the question. Share the small observation. Volunteer for the thing. Trust and reputation get built early, by people who show up, not by people who do the work silently."
ON-SCREEN: "First 90 days = your reputation" → "Don't go quiet" → "Contribute one thing a week"
B-ROLL: desk setup / calendar / coffee
CAPTION: How to start a new job strong. #newgrad #firstjob #careertok #worktok

## DAY 7 — 'Professional' decoded
HOOK: "Employers say Gen Z 'isn't professional.' Here's what they actually mean."
VOICEOVER: "Professionalism sounds stuffy and vague, so let me translate. It means, do you reply to emails. Do you follow through when you say you will. Do you show up on time and prepared. Do you handle feedback without taking it personally. That's it. It's not about being fake or corporate. It's a handful of habits, and every one of them is learnable. Nobody taught you, so learn it now and you're instantly ahead of most people your age."
ON-SCREEN: "Professional = habits, not a personality" → "Reply. Follow through. Show up." → "All learnable"
B-ROLL: phone / email / clock
CAPTION: 'Be more professional' actually means this. #newgrad #careeradvice #worktok

## DAY 8 — The skill AI can't fake
HOOK: "In a job market flooded with AI, here's the one thing that makes you stand out."
VOICEOVER: "Every candidate now has AI writing their materials and prepping their answers. So sounding polished isn't special anymore, everyone sounds polished. The one thing AI can't fake is you. Your real story. Your judgment. The way you make an interviewer trust you when they go off script. That's the skill getting more valuable in this market, not less. Build the human part, because that's the part that's actually scarce now."
ON-SCREEN: "Everyone's polished now" → "AI can't fake YOU" → "The human part is scarce"
B-ROLL: chatbot / interview table
CAPTION: When everyone uses AI, being human is the edge. #jobsearch #ai #newgrad #careertok

## DAY 9 — Networking without the cringe
HOOK: "You don't need to 'network.' You need to do this instead."
VOICEOVER: "Networking feels gross because everyone treats it like begging for a job. Don't. Just be genuinely curious about people's work. Ask one good question and actually listen. Follow up with a specific thank you. That's it. In a world where everyone sends the same AI-written message, being a real, warm, specific human is a superpower. That's how opportunities actually come, through people who remember you, not applications sitting in a pile."
ON-SCREEN: "Networking isn't begging" → "Be curious. Ask. Follow up." → "Being human = the edge"
B-ROLL: coffee chat / phone message / LinkedIn-style scroll
CAPTION: How to network without feeling gross. #jobsearch #newgrad #careeradvice

## DAY 10 — Pick one skill (CLOSE + CTA)
HOOK: "You can't fix everything before your next interview. So don't. Do this."
VOICEOVER: "The job market handed new grads a skills gap that isn't your fault. COVID, no training, AI. But you don't fix it by trying to become perfect overnight. Pick one skill. Telling your story. Speaking up. Presence. Work on just that this week and use it in your next conversation. One rep beats waiting until you feel ready, and most people never even start. I put free guides on all of it in my bio. Start with one."
ON-SCREEN: "The gap isn't your fault" → "Don't fix everything" → "Pick ONE skill. Use it this week."
B-ROLL: sunrise / checklist / walking forward POV
CAPTION: Pick one skill before your next interview. Free guides in bio. #newgrad #jobsearch #careertok

# CapCut recap (per video, ~10 min)
1. Record the script as a **voiceover** on your voice-memo app (no face).
2. New CapCut project → add a **background** (solid color or the day's B-roll).
3. Import the voiceover as your main track.
4. **Auto captions** (Text → Auto captions) → big bold font, center/upper third (not the bottom, buttons cover it).
5. Add the **HOOK** as big text for the first 2–3 seconds.
6. Drop in the **on-screen text beats** at the arrows.
7. Add a **trending sound at ~10% volume** under your voice.
8. Trim under **45 seconds**. Export 1080x1920 → post.
