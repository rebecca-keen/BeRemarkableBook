**The through-line:** the people entering the workforce now lost their people-skills years to COVID, then walked into AI doing the easy tasks they'd normally learn on. The result is a real human-skills gap that is NOT their fault, and every one of those skills can be built. That is the story these 10 videos tell.

**Format:** faceless. You record the script as a voiceover (audio only), then lay it over big on-screen text + simple visuals in CapCut. No face required.

**Cadence:** 1 per day for 10 days. Post around the same time daily (early evening tends to work). Pin your favorite. Reply to every comment for the first hour.

**Every video ends the same way:** a light nudge to "free guides in my bio" (bio link = beremarkablebook.com/links).

# CapCut workflow (faceless, ~10 min per video)

Do this once and it becomes muscle memory.

1. **Record the voiceover.** On your phone's voice memo app, read the script for that day. Talk like you mean it, a little fast. Re-record until one take feels natural. (No face, just audio.)
2. **New project in CapCut.** Import a background: a solid color, or 15–20 seconds of free B-roll (CapCut has a stock library, or use the day's B-roll suggestion). Loop/trim it to cover the whole clip.
3. **Add your voiceover.** Import the audio. This is your main track.
4. **Auto-captions.** Tap Text → Auto captions → it transcribes your voiceover automatically. Then style them: big bold font, white text with a dark outline or box, placed in the **center or upper third** (NOT the bottom, TikTok's buttons cover it).
5. **Add the HOOK as its own big text** on screen for the first 2–3 seconds. This is the single most important element.
6. **Drop in the ON-SCREEN TEXT beats** at the right moments (the arrows in each script mark where).
7. **Add a trending sound at low volume** (5–10%) under your voiceover. Using a trending audio helps the algorithm; keep it quiet so your voice is clear.
8. **Trim to under 45 seconds.** Cut dead air. Tighter is better.
9. **Export** at 1080x1920 (9:16), then upload to TikTok.
